Celebrate with India

Every month brings a new festival — colour, light, music, and devotion.

October / November

Diwali

Festival of Lights. Lakhs of earthen lamps illuminate the night as India celebrates the triumph of light over darkness.

February / March

Holi

Festival of Colours. A riot of gulal powder transforms streets into living rainbows welcoming spring's arrival.

November

Pushkar Fair

World's largest camel fair. Thousands of camels, folk artists, and pilgrims converge under Rajasthan's desert sky.

December

Hornbill Festival

The Festival of Festivals in Nagaland. Sixteen tribes showcase war dances, crafts, and cuisine in Kisama village.

August / September

Onam

Kerala's harvest festival. Floral carpets, Vallam Kali snake boat races, and a 26-course Sadya feast await.

October

Durga Puja

Kolkata transforms into an open-air art gallery as pandals showcase stunning handcrafted tableaux of the goddess.
